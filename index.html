One stroke 一笔

Every Chinese character is written in a fixed order of strokes. Here each character has been written as one line without lifting the pen. You don't need to read Chinese to play: look at the strokes of each option and find the one that matches the line.

一right
丨down
丿down-left
丶down-right
乛right + down,
then all turn 90°

乛 is the only stroke that turns. After it, "right" and "down" rotate 90°, as if you turned the paper and kept drawing. A dot marks where the pen starts.
